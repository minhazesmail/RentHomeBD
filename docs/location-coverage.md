# Search coverage

The shared catalog covers all 64 districts and 503 upazila search centers across Bangladesh, alongside the existing neighborhoods and landmarks. It supplies landing autocomplete, full-map search and owner address matching. Coordinates are approximate search centers, not administrative boundaries, official headquarters or property addresses. The landing search starts at 5 km; users can adjust the full map/radius. Available results still require real, moderated listings. No claim is made that each area already has rental inventory.

English/Bangla names and common transliterations are searchable. Canonical district names precede upazila aliases, and upazila suggestions include the district. Ambiguous bare names such as Kaliganj require a qualified choice instead of silently selecting one. Full addresses prefer the longest whole location phrase; tied matches remain unresolved. Short acronyms only match directly, avoiding DU matching Dubai or DUET. Existing curated centers and URL labels are preserved.

## Nationwide catalog and updates

`src/lib/bangladesh-locations.json` is generated by `node scripts/import-bangladesh-locations.mjs`. The generator downloads a pinned revision, validates counts, parent relationships and coordinate bounds, and adds the documented 2026 supplement. Production needs no geocoding service or runtime catalog download.

- Baseline: [Bangladesh Administrative Divisions](https://github.com/open-admin-data/bangladesh-administrative-divisions/tree/1731ee8585a32ad62f5c898f8e8e3f7659b1aa5a), copyright 2026 jakkrapongt, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). 64 districts, 495 upazilas. Adaptations: stripped unused fields, qualified names, English/Bangla aliases, preserved curated centers and added approved units. Dataset license is retained in `docs/licenses/bangladesh-administrative-divisions.txt`.
- Cross-check: [Bangladesh National Portal district list](https://bangladesh.gov.bd/views/district-list/District-List/) and [upazila list](https://bangladesh.gov.bd/views/upazila-list/Upazilla-List/). The portal currently reports 500; the baseline still contains 495. Neither includes all eight recent approvals in one complete, coordinated release. Consequently the UI says “all 64 districts” rather than asserting a national official upazila count.
- [BSS, May 7, 2026](https://www.bssnews.net/news-flash/385117): Mokamtala (Bogura), Matamuhuri (Cox's Bazar), Ruhea and Bhully (Thakurgaon), Chandraganj (Lakshmipur).
- [BSS, July 1, 2026](https://www.bssnews.net/bangla/news-flash/319928): Fatikchhari Uttar (Chattogram), Bangara (Cumilla), Dakshin Gafargaon (Mymensingh). These are included as searchable places, even while administrative directories catch up. `approved-2026-*` IDs are internal identifiers, not government codes.
